Why your routine isn't working
Six things happening
to your skin right now
You're not doing anything wrong — you just don't have the information. Nobody does.
01
Your products are secretly at war
Retinol + AHA destroys your skin barrier. Vitamin C + Niacinamide cancel each other. Benzoyl peroxide + retinol makes retinol completely useless. These are extremely common combinations that millions of people use daily — because nobody told them not to. Every day you use them together, you're spending money on a reaction instead of results.
02
That "clean" label is a lie
The EU bans 1,600+ cosmetic ingredients. The US bans fewer than 40. Brands freely write "natural," "non-toxic," "fragrance-free" with zero legal definition. Synthetic fragrance is hidden as just "parfum" — one word that can legally contain hundreds of chemicals. You're paying a 40–60% premium for "clean" products that aren't clean at all.
03
You break out on the same days every month
You change your cleanser. You change your toner. You buy new products. Nothing works — because the problem was never your products. The luteal phase of your cycle causes a progesterone spike that increases oil production in a completely predictable pattern. You've been solving the wrong problem for years.
04
Your routine ignores the world outside
Using AHA on a high-UV morning increases your photosensitivity by 50% — actively causing the sun damage you're trying to prevent. On a humid day, your rich moisturiser is congesting your pores. On a polluted day, your skin needs antioxidants before SPF. Your routine is the same every single day regardless of what's happening outside.
05
Your progress photos prove nothing
You're 3 months into tretinoin. You put your week-1 and week-12 photos side by side. Different light. Different angle. Different expression. The comparison is completely useless and you genuinely cannot tell if your skin improved — even if it visibly did. Inconsistent photos are not evidence.
06
You keep buying what already failed you
Average person spends ₹18,000+ a year on skincare. Most of it sits unfinished on a shelf. No memory of what you tried, why you stopped, what pattern keeps repeating. You've bought the same type of Vitamin C serum four times. You just didn't remember the last three stung you.
